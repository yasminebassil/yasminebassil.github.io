08 What to align

The decisions that would bring the whole site onto one system, roughly in order of how visible they are.

  1. Pick one purple. Choose between the site purple #6B5B8E and the in-between trial #6C618A, then replace the cv's #7671C4 with it. The cv is the page recruiters read, and it is currently the furthest from the rest.
  2. Pick one body font. Satoshi is on four pages and DM Sans on three. Moving thoughts, creative and defense to Satoshi is the smaller change.
  3. Use one nav everywhere. Home and bio have the small mono capitals; the other pages still have the older lowercase nav, so the header visibly changes as you click around.
  4. Decide what terracotta is for. It is a hover colour on home, the main accent on creative, and on research and thoughts the variable with that name is really a grey.
  5. Use one Arabic font. IBM Plex Sans Arabic on bio, Noto Sans Arabic elsewhere.
  6. Use one base text size. 17px on home and bio, 16px on the rest.
  7. Decide on the homepage particle colour. #766FE2 matches the brain photo rather than the site purple. Keeping it is reasonable, as long as it is a deliberate exception.

09 Colour check and three directions

A check of the current colours against standard practice, then three palettes that aim for fresh but grounded and mature. Contrast ratios are measured against Paper (#FEFBFD); the accessibility standard (WCAG AA) asks for at least 4.5:1 for normal-size text and 3:1 for large text.

  • Text needs enough contrast. Anything used for small text or links should reach 4.5:1 against the background. Two current colours do not.
  • Roughly 60 / 30 / 10. About 60% neutral background, 30% text and structure, 10% accent. The accent reads as confident when it is rare.
  • One accent means one thing. A single colour for links and emphasis, so readers learn that colour = clickable or important. A second colour only for small labels.
  • Depth reads as maturity. Darker, slightly desaturated colours look more grounded than bright ones; freshness comes from a clean near-white background and one clear hue, not from brightness.
  • Colour is never the only signal. Links keep an underline as well as a colour, which your site already does.
