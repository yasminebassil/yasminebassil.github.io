02 Education & Training

I've moved back and forth between the US and Lebanon throughout my early life, ultimately settling in Atlanta in 2012.

I'm a cognitive neuroscientist who recently completed my PhD in Neuroscience at Emory University. As an NSF Graduate Research Fellow, I worked in the Neural Plasticity Research Lab to study how aging changes the brain's navigation systems using neuroimaging, neurostimulation, and virtual reality.

Over the course of my PhD, I built a new core research capacity for the lab from the ground up — a navigation research program integrating clinical assessments, VR, and neuroimaging to study spatial navigation across the lifespan — tracking milestones and deliverables across 15+ team members, 110+ participants, and 5+ lab partnerships. For more on this work, see research.

Prior to my PhD, I completed my undergraduate degree at the Georgia Institute of Technology, as well as my undergraduate thesis with Dr. Shella Keilholz in the Keilholz MIND Lab, analyzing resting-state brain connectivity in individuals with ADHD, PTSD, and sleep disorders.

05 Personal

The science and the scientist are inextricably intertwined.

Being Lebanese is a central part of my identity, and I try to visit home (Smar Jbeil, Batroun, Lebanon) to see friends and family at least once a year. I always find a piece of Lebanon wherever I go — cooking family recipes, scavenging local record stores for Arabic music, leading the dabke line, or finding small pockets of community in whatever city I'm in.

I love to cook, usually experimenting with dishes from all around the world. I'm an avid plant enthusiast and caretaker to 30+ indoor plants. When I'm exploring the city (by foot, bike, or public transit, preferably), you can usually find me at the discount section of my local plant store, thrift stores, book stores, or speciality grocery stores.

I'm a lover of all things creative. I'm currently part of la brat band, on piano and lead vocals (and occasionally bass!), putting my classical training to good use. We had our debut show last year. Otherwise, I love artistic endeavors in all forms, with my new favorite medium of pottery.
