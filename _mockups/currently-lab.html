Currently lab

Six ways to set the “Currently” section of the bio, all with the same eight facts.

ANow: four-column grid of small labels

On the bio page now. Tidy, but it reads like a spec sheet.

BSoft cards

Each fact on its own small card, with the label in italic mulberry. Friendlier, and it echoes the quote bubble in section 01.

CQuestions and answers

The labels become questions in italic serif, as if someone were interviewing you. The most conversational.

DOne running paragraph

No labels or boxes: a short paragraph in your voice with the key words picked out in mulberry, like the intro.

Right now I'm reading Queer World Making by Andrew Gayed. My favorite flavors are cardamom, jasmine, and rose, and my favorite ingredient to cook with is pomegranate molasses. I recently had the best cocktail of my life: gin, bergamot liqueur, sake, and matcha milk jam. My newest hobbies are pottery and block printing, I'm currently obsessed with learning to count from 1 to 10 in as many languages as I can, and my biggest time sink is color-coding my digital and physical life.

EBig type, two columns

The answers set large in the display serif under small sage labels, like a magazine sidebar.

FOne at a time, with arrows

The same flip-through as the quotes in section 04. Compact, but it hides seven of the eight facts at any moment, and it would be a second flipper right after the first.
