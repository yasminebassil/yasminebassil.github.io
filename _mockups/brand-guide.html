Brand guide

The colours, typefaces, and small rules the site is built on, as they stand today. The values themselves live in css/site.css; change them there and every page that uses the tokens follows.

01 Colours

Oxblood, mulberry, and sage on porcelain, with slate violet kept for blocks. Any text colour should keep at least 4.5:1 contrast against its background.

Oxblood

#44181D · --oxblood

Headlines, the wordmark, numbers on cards, the main filled blocks.

Mulberry

#6E3358 · --mulberry

Links, buttons, section labels, and words picked out in a sentence. The one interactive colour.

Sage

#566B58 · --sage

The status badge, small secondary labels, and a block colour.

Slate violet

#4F5285 · --slate

Blocks and illustration only. Never text: it reads too close to mulberry.

Blush

#F3E6E3 · --blush

Soft panels, the quote bubble, tags, and badges.

Porcelain

#FCFAF8 · --paper

The page, and the sheet a page's content sits on. Matches the sky in the homepage photo.

Warm grey

#F1EFEC · --paper-warm

The “desk” behind a sheet, and quiet panels.

Warm ink

#2B2224 · --ink

Body text that needs full strength: side-column facts, bold opening sentences.

Warm grey text

#5E5557 · --muted

Running paragraphs, the menu, and secondary text.

Hairlines: rgba(43, 34, 36, 0.14) as --rule, and 0.07 as --rule-soft. Status dot: #A9D6A4.

02 Typefaces

Fraunces

The wordmark, page headlines, the menu, card titles, and big numbers. Always with SOFT 45 and WONK 1. Lowercase for the wordmark and menu; weight 600 for headlines, 500 for the menu.

Building the structures and systems
font-family: 'Fraunces' · font-variation-settings: 'SOFT' 45, 'WONK' 1

A section's opening line, and quotes in italic. Not for body text or labels.

04 Layout
  • Content sits on a porcelain sheet (12px corners, a very soft shadow) on the warm grey desk.
  • A section is a wide text column with a narrow side column of labelled facts beside it.
  • Generous space between sections; white space is part of the look.
  • Movement is used sparingly and only where a visitor can stop it.
05 Writing
  • The wordmark and menu are lowercase: “yasmine bassil”.
  • Use the Oxford comma: “programs, partnerships, tools, and teams”.
  • Write numbers as numerals: “6 years”, “3 organizations”, “$260K+”.
  • Say “scientific research” where the point is broader than neuroscience.
  • Plain, first-person sentences; avoid résumé phrasing and wording that dates the work (“recently completed”).
